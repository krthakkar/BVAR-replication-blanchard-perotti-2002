# BVAR-replication-blanchard-perotti-2002
Bayesian fiscal SVAR based on Blanchard and Perotti (2002), with Minnesota priors and structural impulse responses to tax and government spending shocks.

## Repository structure

- **`Data`** — Input data and elasticity series used in the estimation.
- **`Code`** — MATLAB scripts for data preparation, prior specification, posterior simulation, and impulse-response computation.
- **`Results`** — Model description, Bayesian estimation details, figures, and tables of posterior impulse responses.

## Reference

Blanchard, O., and Perotti, R. (2002). “An Empirical Characterization of the Dynamic Effects of Changes in Government Spending and Taxes on Output.” *The Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 117(4), 1329–1368.
